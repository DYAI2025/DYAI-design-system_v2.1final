8 · Focus, motion, flatness

Focus is a 2px cobalt outline at 3px offset on every interactive element, visible on pearl and inside recesses. prefers-reduced-motion collapses all transitions and the processing pulse; progress is still communicated by width and stage ticks. Under forced-colors and with shadows disabled, tonal steps (porcelain → raised → recessed) keep convex/concave distinguishable without any shadow — the flatness test.
